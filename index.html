Gestures Quick Guide

  • Grab Pipe: Pinch thumb & index fingertip near the pipe.
  • Drag: Move your hand to move the hose naturally with physics.
  • Smoke: Bring mouthpiece to your mouth. Water bubbles & coals flare!
  • Exhale: Move the pipe away and open your mouth to release thick clouds of smoke.
  • Smoke Rings: Exhale while making an "O" shape with your lips.
  • Change Flavor: Open-palm swipe left or right in front of camera.
